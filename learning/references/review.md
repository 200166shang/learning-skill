# Review

Review is manual, retrieval-first, and evidence-backed. Prefer targets in this order: active or repaired misconceptions, earlier strong-hint passes, least recently reviewed questions, then ordinary verified questions.

Ask one small recall, explanation, application, or transfer prompt before showing notes. Record the result as `kind: review` with independence, demonstrated capabilities, and gaps.

Never reactivate a closed Episode or reopen its historical questions. A failed review records current evidence while preserving history. If the learner chooses to relearn, start a new Episode that may reference the historical question.

Do not schedule automatic reviews and do not add FSRS or numeric mastery scores.
